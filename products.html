High-Purity Silanes

Four organofunctional silane grades engineered for adhesion, coupling, and crosslinking across coatings, composites, rubber, solar, and wire & cable. Every grade is supplied with a Certificate of Analysis, MSDS, and technical data sheet.

SMB-CL-150

Methacryloxy Silane

A high-purity methacryloxy-functional silane that couples mineral and glass surfaces to unsaturated polyester and acrylic polymers, improving mechanical strength, wet-strength retention, and dispersion.

Coupling Agent Composites Solar PV

Chemical name3-Methacryloxypropyltrimethoxysilane
CAS number2530-85-8

AppearanceClear, colourless liquid
Key functionsCoupling agent, adhesion promoter, crosslinker
Typical applicationsUnsaturated polyester and acrylic composites, mineral-filled thermosets and thermoplastics, glass-fibre sizing, adhesives, coatings and inks, solar (photovoltaic) module coatings and encapsulants
